Are you looking forward to starting your professional career in a multinational environment? Join our internship program to launch your career with us and benefit from our hands-on approach. We are looking for candidates available for 1 year internship as of March 2027. Your responsibilities As a Young Talent Simulation, you will contribute to optimising engine components and gain hands-on experience in simulation, analysis, and technical reporting within an expert team.

  • You carry out technical calculations on internal combustion engine components and assemblies
  • You carry out structural calculations, including static, dynamic, linear, and non-linear FEM analyses
  • You assess component fatigue life
  • You evaluate the results of simulations
  • You implement and improve the evaluation methods of fatigue test results
  • You write technical reports
  • You support the simulation team and benefit from their experience to start your career Your profile
  • You hold a degree in Mechanical Engineering or an equivalent qualification.
  • You have a good understanding of and interest in Finite Element Method (FEM) simulation and strength of materials.
  • You have at least a basic understanding of material fatigue theory.
  • You are familiar with FEM software (e.g. ANSYS) and proficient in MS Office.Programming experience would be an asset.
  • You are fluent in English and/or French, German is a plus.

Ai-je besoin d'un permis de frontalier pour un poste dans le canton de Fribourg ?

Les citoyens UE/AELE résidant dans un État UE/AELE peuvent demander un permis G et travailler dans toute la Suisse : les zones frontalières ne s'appliquent plus à eux, et ils doivent rentrer au moins une fois par semaine. L'employeur suisse dépose la demande auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Fribourg après signature du contrat. Les délais varient selon le canton — confirmez les détails auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Fribourg ou avec les RH pendant la candidature.
