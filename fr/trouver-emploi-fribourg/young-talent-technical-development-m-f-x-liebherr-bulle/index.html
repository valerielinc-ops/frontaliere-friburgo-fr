Description complète

Are you looking for an opportunity to gain hands-on experience in engine development within an international industrial environment? Join Liebherr Machines Bulle SA as a Young Talent - Technical Development and contribute to the development of combustion engines for defense applications and gain hands-on experience in a challenging international engineering environment. We are looking for a candidate available for 12 months internship as of February 2027. Your responsibilities

  • You support technical development projects for combustion engines dedicated to defense applications, from concept development to validation and integration.
  • You assist in the coordination and follow-up of engineering activities, ensuring alignment with project requirements, timelines and deliverables.
  • You contribute to the analysis and implementation of technical and customer requirements.
  • You support the development and application of engineering tools and methodologies, including reliability analysis, continuous improvement and technical documentation.
  • You collaborate with cross-functional teams, including simulation, electronics, testing and other engineering departments.
  • You participate in technical investigations, problem-solving activities and product improvement initiatives.
  • You support the preparation of technical reports, project documentation and presentations. Your profile
  • You are currently pursuing or recently completed a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Automotive Engineering, Industrial Engineering or a related technical field.
  • You have a strong interest in combustion engines, mechanical systems and technical development.
  • You have good analytical and problem-solving skills, with a structured and detail-oriented approach.
  • You are proactive, curious and motivated to learn in a multidisciplinary engineering environment.
  • You are fluent in English, knowledge of French and/or German is an asset.
  • You have good knowledge of MS Office; familiarity with engineering or project management tools is an advantage. This position is subject to a background check process; the process is conducted by our partner Aequivalent. Avons-nous suscité votre intérêt ? Ai-je besoin d'un permis de frontalier pour un poste dans le canton de Fribourg ?

Les citoyens UE/AELE résidant dans un État UE/AELE peuvent demander un permis G et travailler dans toute la Suisse : les zones frontalières ne s'appliquent plus à eux, et ils doivent rentrer au moins une fois par semaine. L'employeur suisse dépose la demande auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Fribourg après signature du contrat. Les délais varient selon le canton — confirmez les détails auprès de l'office des migrations du canton de Fribourg ou avec les RH pendant la candidature.
